Night & morning

Night mode
From bedtime until the wake-up time, only the time shows, in dim red or amber on black. Tap the screen to see everything for a few seconds.
Bedtime
Night mode starts here. A bedtime countdown shows in the evening.
Wake-up time
Used on days with no alarm. On alarm days, the earliest alarm is the wake-up time.
Night color
Night brightness
A web page can't turn down the backlight. Also set the iPad's own brightness low (see README).
"OK to get up" light
At wake-up time the screen glows green with a sun for 45 minutes. Red means stay in bed, green means get up. Works with silent alarms too.
Preview night mode
Closes Settings and shows the night face for 10 seconds.

Talking clock

Talking clock
Tap the time to hear it said out loud, handy before kids can read a clock. Alarms start with a spoken good morning and the day, and it says good night at bedtime. Uses the iPad's built-in voice, not Siri, and works offline.
Hear it
Says what a tap on the time would say right now.
